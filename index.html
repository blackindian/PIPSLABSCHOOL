Before you register

Find your server
  1. Open your Exness Personal Area and click your trading account.
  2. Copy the server name in the account details. It looks like Exness-MT5Trial8 (demo) or Exness-MT5Real8 (real).
  3. In the MT5 app, see File, then Login to Trade Account.
Set your investor password
  1. In the Personal Area, open the settings for your trading account.
  2. Choose set or change investor password and save it.
  3. Enter that password here, never your trading password. Menu names can change, so check Exness Help.
